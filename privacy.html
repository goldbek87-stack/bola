Oxirgi yangilanish: 2026-yil 27-oktabr

“Bilim va Mukofot” — ota-onalar bolalariga o’quv topshiriqlari va uy ishlari berib, bajarilganini tekshiradigan va ball hamda mukofotni hisoblab boradigan ilova. Ushbu siyosat ilova qanday ma’lumotlarni yig’ishi, nimaga ishlatishi va qanday himoya qilishini tushuntiradi.

Qisqacha: ilova reklama ko’rsatmaydi, joylashuv, kamera, mikrofon va kontaktlarga kirmaydi, ma’lumotlarni sotmaydi. Haqiqiy pul to’lovlari yo’q — “mukofot” faqat ilova ichidagi hisob-kitob.

1. Kimlar foydalanadi

Hisobni faqat ota-ona yoki vasiy (18 yosh va undan katta) ochadi. Bola o’z hisobini yaratmaydi: bola profilini ota-ona qo’shadi, bola esa ota-ona belgilagan 4 raqamli PIN bilan kiradi. Biz 18 yoshgacha bo’lgan shaxslardan to’g’ridan-to’g’ri ma’lumot so’ramaymiz va ularga maqsadli reklama ko’rsatmaymiz.

2. Qanday ma’lumotlar yig’iladi

  • Ota-ona hisobi: elektron pochta manzili va parol (parol shifrlangan holda saqlanadi, biz uni ko’ra olmaymiz).
  • Bola profili (ota-ona kiritadi): ism yoki taxallus, avatar (emoji), sinf, PIN kod, ota-onaning izohi, ball narxi.
  • Ilova ichidagi faoliyat: topshiriqlar, uy ishlari, ularning holati, to’plangan ball va mukofot summasi, o’quv statistikasi (fanlar bo’yicha daraja va javoblar).
  • Qurilmada saqlanadigan ma’lumotlar: yuqoridagilarning nusxasi qurilma xotirasida ham saqlanadi. Ota-ona zaxira nusxa (JSON) faylini o’zi yuklab olishi va tiklashi mumkin.

Ilova joylashuv, kamera, mikrofon, kontaktlar, fotosuratlar, reklama identifikatori kabi ma’lumotlarni yig’maydi. Ovozli talaffuz funksiyasi qurilmaning matnni ovozga aylantirish imkoniyatidan foydalanadi, ovoz yozib olinmaydi.

3. Nimaga ishlatiladi

Ma’lumotlar faqat ilovaning asosiy vazifalari uchun ishlatiladi: hisobga kirish, bolaga topshiriq va uy ishi berish, natijani tekshirish, ball va mukofotni hisoblash, ota-ona va bola qurilmalarini sinxronlash. Ma’lumotlar reklama, profilash yoki sotish uchun ishlatilmaydi.

4. Xizmat ko’rsatuvchilar va uchinchi tomonlar

  • Supabase — hisob (autentifikatsiya) va bulutli ma’lumotlar bazasi. Ma’lumotlar HTTPS orqali shifrlangan holda uzatiladi. Supabase biz nomimizdan ma’lumotlarni saqlovchi xizmat ko’rsatuvchi sifatida ishlaydi.
  • jsDelivr va cdnjs — ilova ba’zi dasturiy kutubxonalarni (Supabase klienti, PDF va Word fayllarni o’qish) ushbu tarqatish tarmoqlaridan yuklaydi. Bunda qurilmaning IP manzili ular serveriga ko’rinishi mumkin; shaxsiy ma’lumotlar yuborilmaydi.

Boshqa hech kimga ma’lumot sotilmaydi va berilmaydi, qonun talab qilgan hollar bundan mustasno.

5. Saqlash muddati va xavfsizlik

Ma’lumotlar hisob mavjud ekan saqlanadi. Ota-ona bola profilini o’chirsa, shu bolaning ma’lumotlari ham o’chiriladi. Ma’lumotlar uzatishda shifrlanadi, bulutdagi jadvallarga kirish hisob egasi bilan cheklangan. Hech bir tizim 100% xavfsiz emas, lekin biz ma’lumotlarni himoya qilish uchun oqilona choralar ko’ramiz.

Bilim va Mukofot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

“Bilim va Mukofot” (“Knowledge and Reward”) is an app where parents assign study tasks and chores to their children, review the results, and track points and rewards. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how it is protected.

In short: the app shows no ads, does not access location, camera, microphone or contacts, and does not sell data. There are no real payments — “rewards” are only an in-app ledger.

1. Who uses the app

Only a parent or legal guardian (18 or older) creates an account. Children do not create accounts: the parent adds a child profile and the child signs in with a 4-digit PIN set by the parent. We do not directly collect data from persons under 18 and do not show them targeted advertising.

2. Data we collect

  • Parent account: email address and password (the password is stored hashed; we cannot read it).
  • Child profile (entered by the parent): name or nickname, avatar (emoji), grade, PIN, parent’s note, point value.
  • In-app activity: tasks, chores, their status, points and reward amounts earned, learning statistics (level and answers per subject).
  • On-device data: a copy of the above is also kept in the device storage. Parents can export and restore a backup (JSON) file themselves.

The app does not collect location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or advertising IDs. The pronunciation feature uses the device’s text-to-speech; no audio is recorded.

3. How we use it

Only to provide the app’s core features: signing in, assigning tasks and chores, reviewing results, calculating points and rewards, and syncing parent and child devices. Data is not used for advertising, profiling or sale.

4. Service providers and third parties

  • Supabase — authentication and cloud database. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Supabase acts as a service provider storing data on our behalf.
  • jsDelivr and cdnjs — the app loads some software libraries (Supabase client, PDF and Word file readers) from these content delivery networks. Your device’s IP address may be visible to them; no personal data is sent.

We do not sell or share data with anyone else, except where required by law.

5. Retention and security

Data is kept while the account exists. If a parent deletes a child profile, that child’s data is deleted too. Data is encrypted in transit and access to cloud tables is restricted to the account owner. No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect data.

6. Access and account deletion

A parent can at any time:

  • edit or delete a child profile and its data in the app;
  • delete the whole account and all related data with the “Delete account” button in the app’s Settings;
  • or email goldbek87@gmail.com from the registered address to request account deletion or a copy of the data. Requests are completed within 30 days.

7. Children’s privacy

If you believe a child’s data was entered without parental consent, contact us and it will be deleted. Child profile data is entered and managed only by the parent; the child cannot communicate with strangers through the app.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page and the date above updated. Questions: goldbek87@gmail.com.
